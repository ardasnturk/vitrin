# Zine Collage (`zine-collage`)

> Vitrin's own preset, distilled from the sticker-heavy galleries of social and dating apps. No app's screenshots or assets were copied.

A photocopied fanzine: hot magenta, yellow and cobalt slides in turn, a halftone fade, a checkerboard strip along the foot, tape on the device and a star sticker. Big condensed caps, tipped 2°, with one phrase in a tilted pill.

## Typography

Anton, uppercase, ~0.15 × width, up to three lines. Body DM Sans.

## Use it when

Social, dating, creators, young audiences. Devices tilt 5°; badges wear as stickers. It is the loudest preset, so keep headlines to three or four words. Never for finance, health or anything that needs trust.
