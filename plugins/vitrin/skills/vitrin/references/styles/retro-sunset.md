# Retro Sunset (`retro-sunset`)

> Vitrin's own preset, distilled from warm, seventies-printed store galleries of music and lifestyle apps. No app's screenshots or assets were copied.

A striped sun rises behind the phone, flat and slightly grainy like an old travel poster. Cream, teal and brick slides take turns under a soft Fraunces headline with one italic word.
