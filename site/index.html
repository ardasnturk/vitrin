What it does

Two jobs. One tool.

Every app needs a good store page and someone to look after it. Vitrin does both.

Make the listing

Claude reads your code and your competitors, then writes and designs everything your store page needs.

  • Name, subtitle, keywords, description, What's New
  • Screenshots for every device, in every language
  • App Previews, creatives and In-App Event cards
  • Checked against the App Store's rules, then uploaded

Run the store

Every app on your team, in one native window. Everything App Store Connect does, without the browser tabs.

  • Versions, builds, TestFlight and reviews
  • Prices, purchases, sales and analytics
  • Users, devices, certificates and Apple Ads
  • Submit, release or phase a version from your desk

How it works

From your code to the store in four steps

Claude does the creative work and you make the choices. Everything else is a tested command you can read.

  1. 1

    Analyze

    Vitrin finds your platforms, languages and features in the code. Every feature points to the file that proves it, so your listing only claims what your app really does.

    Swift, Flutter, React Native and Expo, detected automatically.

  2. 2

    Research

    It searches the App Store in each country, reads competitors' screenshots with on-device OCR and scores keywords by relevance, popularity and competition.

    You pick the platforms and languages. Nothing is assumed.

  3. 3

    Create

    Claude recreates your screens from the code, with no build and no simulator. Then Vitrin renders the screenshots and videos, and you choose from real samples of your own app.

    Need real captures? It can also build and shoot your app in simulators.

  4. 4

    Review and upload

    You see everything in one gallery and approve it, or reject a single item with a comment. Vitrin uploads exactly what you approved and reads back what the store saved.

    The asc CLI does the uploading, so there is no Ruby to install.

Safety

Nothing reaches the store without your yes

Vitrin is built to be trusted with a live listing.

  • Truthful by designEvery headline and claim must point to a feature in your code. Ratings come from the live App Store, and testimonials only from your own reviews.
  • Caught before Apple doesOne command checks text, images and videos against the App Store's rules, and tells Claude exactly what to fix.
  • Approval tied to the filesYour approval records a hash of every file. If anything changes afterwards, even by Claude, it needs a new approval.
  • Partial uploads stay partialA dry-run plan comes first. What you didn't select is never deleted or overwritten, and every upload is read back.

FAQ

Good questions

Does it work with Flutter or React Native apps?

Yes. Swift and SwiftUI come first, and Flutter, React Native and Expo projects are detected automatically. Their screens are recreated from the code without a build, or built with their own tools and captured in simulators if you prefer. Vitrin is for the App Store, so only their Apple apps are covered.

Do I need an OpenAI or Anthropic API key?

No. The creative work happens inside your Claude Code session, and the CLI makes no AI calls. Without Claude you can follow the briefs by hand.

Can I use it from my iPhone?

Yes. With Remote Code 1.6 on your Mac, Vitrin is a tab on your iPhone. You can add project folders, run every stage and manage App Store Connect in native screens, and Vitrin for Mac doesn't have to be open. Projects stay in step between the phone and the Mac.

Do I need the terminal for the Mac app?

No. The welcome tour checks your Mac and installs Node.js, the Vitrin CLI, Claude Code, the asc CLI and ffmpeg with a click. It uses Homebrew if you have it and works without it.

Can I connect App Store Connect without creating a key by hand?

Yes. Sign in with your Apple Account and Vitrin creates an Admin API key in one step. You can also add a key yourself, or use the login you already have in the asc CLI. Projects work without any connection.

Can it create a new app in App Store Connect?

Vitrin for Mac can, once you sign in with your Apple Account. Apple doesn't allow it with an API key, so the CLI and plugin stop and tell you when the app is missing.

Will it upload anything without asking?

No. Every image, video and text is shown to you first. The upload starts with a dry-run plan, needs your yes, and refuses content that differs from what you approved. Submitting for review needs its own confirmation. In the plugin, prices, releases and the age rating and privacy questionnaires stay with you.

What exactly gets uploaded?

Only the platforms and content you selected, after your approval. Selected screenshot sets are replaced per display type, and everything else stays as it is. Vitrin never uploads a build.

Does it need to build my app?

No. By default Claude recreates your screens in HTML from the source code, so there's no build, no simulator and no demo mode. If you'd rather have real captures, it can build your app and open each screen in dedicated simulators.

What happens to the generated files?

They stay in your project's metadata/ folder, and a managed .gitignore block keeps them out of git. After a checked upload, Vitrin asks whether to delete the research data. Nothing else is deleted.

What about licenses?

Vitrin is MIT licensed. It bundles OFL fonts and Lucide icons, and the style presets are adapted from ParthJadhav/app-store-screenshots (MIT). App Previews are rendered with Remotion, which is free for individuals and companies with up to three employees. Larger teams need a Remotion company license.

Is this an Apple product?

No. Vitrin is an independent open-source project, and it isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Apple Inc.
